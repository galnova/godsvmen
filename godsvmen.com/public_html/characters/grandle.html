Introduction

Born with the extraordinary Midas touch, Grandle's mother was *redacted*. He has an older sister, Shana, who is both a renowned Sword Master and the only female General in the Robe Army. Grandle is the sole male heir to the Robe Kingdom, is the son of Crin Robinson, the Robe King.

Despite his youth, Grandle is dedicated to mastering control over his unique ability. His aim is to harness this power effectively in combat, turning his once-cursed gift into a strategic advantage. A handpicked Robe soldier named Yosh was assigned to both train and protect young Grandle as a Royal Guardian.

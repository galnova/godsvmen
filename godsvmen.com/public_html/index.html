The World of GVM

Earth

A Survived Earth, 10,000 years after we're all gone.

Continents

Ottom

The Military Stronghold The land where all non-magic weapons are manufactured. All military arms are forged here. Ottom is known for its advanced weapons and vehicle technology. With huge weapons contracts with every other nation, it keeps the balance of war and peace.

Abya Yala

The Fight Kingdom The Robe family home. A country created by immigrants from all other countries who wanted to find their own way. Constant tournaments are held for money, fame and prestige instead of constant war. Satellite stadiums exist all over the world and anyone can enter.

Lune

The Home of the Wolves The land of natural riches. Lune’s infinite moon energy is other-worldly. The ancient wolves that settled here made sure the land would always be rich in food, clean water and natural resources. Trade is strong, but many profits go to Lore and the AllKing.

Lore

The Country of Magic The Weber’s home country. It’s home to the biggest united army. Weber’s Lit Knights and Dover’s Eln Acolytes reside here. The birthplace of magic sets the rules for magical creatures, enchanted weapons, and general magic usage around the world. Many towns in Lore float.

Hanor

Land of GOD Hanor is the God Dover’s land. He summons the AllKing once a year and demands tributes. This ritual is called God’s Call and usually involves a sadistic act and human sacrifices.

Gradiis

Goth Country Gradiis is the land of the Goths and the only land the Gods have no reign over. Far away across a vast ocean, very few have ever seen it. They don’t have known magic, but their land is rich in both warriors and resources.

The Story Thus Far!!!

The story starts with the mysterious disappearance of DOVER, the God of Swords, and the self-actualization of Sol, the AllKing. This raises suspicions of foul play in the world. With DOVER's relentless pursuit for the truth behind the vanishing monarch, will the truth be revealed, or will darkness prevail in this high-stakes battle of destiny?

The Forever Fight

Arm Yourselves Fighters gear up for upcoming battles by refining their skills, crafting meticulous strategies, and unleashing their full potential. Embracing the challenge, they focus on personal growth, and aim for victory in a display of unwavering determination and skill.
